# Damaged or defective items

- If an item arrives damaged or defective and the customer reports it within
  14 days of delivery, they get a full refund for the damaged items. They do
  not need to send the item back.
- Spoiled or contaminated food counts as damaged.
- If it is unclear which item is damaged or how many, ask the customer before
  issuing a refund.
- Damage reported more than 14 days after delivery cannot be refunded by a
  support agent and must be escalated.
- If the customer says their pet became ill or was injured, escalate
  immediately and advise them to contact their veterinarian. Never give
  medical advice.
