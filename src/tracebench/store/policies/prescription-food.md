# Prescription food

- Prescription diets require authorization from the pet's veterinarian before
  purchase.
- Support agents must not issue refunds or process returns for prescription
  items, even if the item is damaged.
- All prescription requests (returns, refunds, damage, or questions about
  whether a diet suits a pet) must be escalated to the pharmacy team.
- Support agents may share order status and stock or restock information for
  prescription items.
- Never give medical or dosage advice.
