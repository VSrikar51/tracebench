# Returns

- Most items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. Gold loyalty
  members get 60 days (see `loyalty`).
- Food, treats, litter, and health products can only be returned if unopened.
  The customer must confirm the item is unopened.
- Toys, beds, furniture, accessories, and aquatics can be returned opened or
  unopened, as long as they are in good condition.
- Prescription food cannot be returned through a support agent
  (see `prescription-food`).
- Orders that were cancelled or have not been delivered cannot be returned.
  See `cancellations` and `shipping`.
- The refund amount follows the `refunds` policy.
