# Refunds

- A refund equals the price paid per unit, as shown on the order, times the
  quantity being refunded.
- A refund can never be more than the order total minus any refunds already
  issued on that order.
- Support agents may issue refunds of up to $100.00 per order. Any refund over
  $100.00 must be escalated, not issued (see `escalation`).
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
- Store credit and gift cards are not offered instead of refunds.
- Every refund must include a reason.
