# Autoship

- Customers can set up Autoship on food, treats, litter, and health products
  to get 5% off every delivery.
- Customers can skip a delivery, change the frequency, or cancel Autoship at
  any time from Account > Autoship.
- Support agents can explain how Autoship works but cannot change a
  customer's subscription.
- Prescription food is not eligible for Autoship.
